Planning for Health

How health services are planned for a population and for an organisation: what planning is and who does it, the social and health system context in Romania, the need, demand and utilisation of services, community needs assessment, the planning process and the planning audit, the business plan, strategic and marketing planning. Four modules of 4 hours each (two in the classroom, two online), each built around a single case; the practical activities, individual and in teams, take place outside class hours, with mentoring on request.

How to study

Three steps every week

  1. 15–25 min

    Before class

    The Before class tab brings together the key ideas, the introductory section and a short pre-class check.

  2. 2 hours

    In class

    Class time is spent on applications, case studies and discussion, not on repeating the notes.

  3. 10–15 min

    After class

    Self-assessment with feedback and spaced repetition consolidate the concepts; the full notes remain the exam reading.

Assessment

How the final grade is built

Pass requirements and reading

The updated electronic lecture notes published on the course website.

Individual examination (50%): an open-book case study on a new county, after Module 4. Team project (30%): the plan of a health service for a county, in three stages, presented in Module 4. Individual portfolio (20%): the community profile (AP1) and the planning audit of an organisation (AP2). The activities during the modules (the votes, the discussions, the module's tool) are not graded. Decision of the course leader, 1.11.2026.
