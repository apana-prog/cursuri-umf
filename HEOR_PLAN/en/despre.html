How the materials are written and checked

The lecture notes are the course's reference source. The slides, practical sessions, self-assessment questions and interactive tools are derived from the notes and checked automatically, so that they contain no statement absent from them.

Every figure points to an entry in the public source register (the Sources and data page), which gives the source, the exact location in it, the reference year of the data, the nature of the check and its date. Statistical data are extracted programmatically from the files published by the issuer and archived on the date of consultation; scientific articles are checked against Crossref, including for retractions.

Before publication, the text goes through a similarity check. Data are checked against their sources every month, and every change is recorded in the version history.

AI assistance statement

These materials were drafted with the assistance of an artificial intelligence model (Claude, Anthropic), from the sources cited, and were checked and approved by the course lead. Every figure has its source, its place in the source and the date of verification listed on the Sources page.

Report an error

Please give the paragraph code (e.g. C03 § p017), the error you found and, if possible, the correct source. Every report is answered, and corrections are recorded in the version history.
